Under the hood

How it works

Every frame from your webcam is captured server-side with OpenCV, passed through MediaPipe's hand landmark model to extract 21 3D keypoints, then run through a lightweight geometric classifier that measures finger extension angles to determine the gesture — all in well under a video frame's worth of time.

  • Python + Flask backend
  • OpenCV frame capture & encoding
  • MediaPipe Hands landmark model
  • Vanilla JS + streaming MJPEG feed
